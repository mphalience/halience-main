Runs across acquired Companies, held positions and launched Ventures in the portfolio.
Hands portfolio_diagnostician the quarter's operating reports, ownership reports, gate outcomes and material changes.
Material means a business off plan or case beyond configured rules, or an intervention opportunity above threshold.
Never compare businesses on raw confidential detail; use agreed measures. Never decide interventions or hold, scale or
exit; route them to the owners.
