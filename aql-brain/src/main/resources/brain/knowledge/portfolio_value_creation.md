# Portfolio value creation (LP-12)

## Purpose
`LP-12` uses post-launch and post-acquisition evidence to diagnose, intervene and learn across businesses. Cycle:
Monitor, diagnose, intervene, measure, learn, reassess. It is a directional later-phase loop and is intentionally not
decomposed into feature detail.

## Scope
Acquired Companies (from the Acquire build), held investment positions (from the Invest build) and launched Ventures
(from the Build build). A Company and a Venture are distinct objects; a Venture may later become associated with a
Company.

## Inputs
Weekly operating reports, ownership reports, investment cases and decision records, venture gate outcomes, operating
health assessments and material change signals.

## Method
1. Monitor through `LP-03`; material changes may need attention (`LP-08`).
2. Diagnose performance and its drivers from evidence; research gaps through `LP-14`.
3. Identify interventions: expected impact, time to impact, dependencies, cost, and Halience's ability to influence.
4. Decide: material interventions and hold, scale or exit decisions are governed human decisions, recorded with basis
   (`LP-10`) and approved through `LP-15` where required.
5. Intervene through `LP-09` work.
6. Measure and learn through `LP-06`; patterns across businesses may feed `LP-13`.

## Frameworks
Business Performance and Financial Health frameworks provide measures and benchmarks once defined. A Portfolio
Performance framework may be distinct or a composition of Business Performance plus investment-specific dimensions;
this is to be confirmed in workshop.

## Boundaries
- Each company's data stays in its own scope. Cross-company comparison uses agreed measures, not raw confidential
  detail, and cross-company learning uses only approved privacy-safe signals (`GV-20`).
- Diagnosis concerns the business, never individual employees (`GV-22`).

## Open items
Portfolio Performance framework scope, intervention materiality thresholds, review cadence, decision owners.
