# Business Performance framework (proposed)

## Status
Workshop discovery. This cross-domain framework will define how Halience evaluates and benchmarks overall company
performance. It is expected to be reusable across portfolio, finance and company assessment. Ownership is shared
between Investment and Finance and is to be confirmed in workshop.

## What it will contain
Per the Domain Evaluation structure: dimension, description, criterion or measure, evaluation method, benchmark or
reference, threshold or rule, weight, evidence required, confidence or sufficiency rule, owner.

## Relationship to other frameworks
- Financial Health is expected to supply the financial dimension rather than be duplicated here.
- A Portfolio Performance framework may be distinct or may be Business Performance plus investment-specific
  dimensions; to be decided.

## Candidate areas (to confirm, not settled)
Commercial performance, customer outcomes, operational delivery, financial performance, people and capability at
organisational level, risk and compliance posture.

## To be decided
- Scope and owner of the framework.
- Dimensions, measures and definitions.
- Benchmarks by sector, stage and size.
- Thresholds and ratings, and which criteria are scored versus judged.
- Weights, if any.
- Evidence requirements and confidence rules.

## Boundary
People and capability are assessed at organisational level only. The framework must not become a means of monitoring
individual employees (`GV-22`).

## Until decided
Consumers show measures and trends with definitions, without ratings, and say the framework is not yet defined.
