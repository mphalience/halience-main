# GTM content rules

These rules govern creation (PA-026) and validation (PA-027) of GTM assets. They are a working first cut; exact
brand guidelines, channel rules and commercial rules are GTM decisions to be confirmed.

## Grounding and claims
- Every factual claim traces to governed evidence or an approved claim in the brief (CX-17). No source, no claim.
- Keep claim classes distinct: sourced fact, modelled or derived figure, and opinion or judgement (GV-02).
- Statistics carry source and date; stale or unverifiable figures are removed or flagged.
- No superlatives or comparative claims about others unless evidenced and approved.
- Product capability claims must match what the product actually does at the stated entitlement depth.

## Independence and trust
- Settle intelligence is independent. Content must not imply that a score, verdict or risk can be bought, softened
  or hidden, and must not imply material risk information is paywalled.
- Performance goals never justify weakening accuracy, independence or trust.

## Regulated perimeter
- No regulated financial, mortgage or legal advice (GV-15). Affordability examples are labelled indicative, with
  assumptions, and never imply lender eligibility, approval or a guarantee (GV-17).
- Jurisdiction-specific statements are dated and scoped to the nation they apply to.

## Privacy
- No personal data, private documents or identifiable user situations without explicit permitted basis (GV-08).
- Testimonials and case studies need recorded consent.

## Brand and persona
- Use the approved persona and voice configuration (CX-15). Persona never overrides truth or safety.
- Plain British English, no hype, no fear-based urgency.

## Commercial and channel
- Pricing, offers and entitlement statements match the current approved commercial terms.
- Channel constraints (length, format, disclosure labels) apply per channel brief.

## Open items
Approved brand guidelines, per-channel rules, approved claims register and the severity scale for validation issues.
