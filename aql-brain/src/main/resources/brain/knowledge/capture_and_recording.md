# Capture and recording (GV-21)

## Principle
Recorded meetings, calls and conversations are governed source material, not automatic organisational truth.
Capture is channel-agnostic and subject to consent, notice, jurisdiction, access and retention rules. Phase 1 is
interface-ready; exact capture channels remain workshop scope.

## Before use
A capture can be processed only when its record shows: participants; consent and notice state; jurisdiction; access
list; retention rule. Unknown or refused consent means hold.

## Status and access
- A capture keeps restricted status and its own access list. Linking it to a Work Context does not widen access.
- Structured items extracted from it inherit the same status and access.
- Extracted items are "stated in the recording", not verified facts. A stated decision is a candidate Decision until
  an authorised person records it; a stated commitment becomes a Commitment only when confirmed.

## Path to Knowledge
Promotion follows `LP-07` (see knowledge_promotion): propose, review, approve, write. Private or restricted material
is never promoted by default.

## Retention and erasure
Retain where lawful and useful; erase, anonymise or minimise where required (`GV-09`).

## Non-surveillance
Processing extracts work state: decisions, commitments, questions, risks, references. It does not measure
individuals' speaking time, tone, sentiment or participation (`GV-22`).

## Open items
Capture channels in scope, consent mechanisms per jurisdiction, default retention periods, who may process captures.
