# Investment lifecycle (LP-18)

## Purpose
`LP-18` moves an opportunity from evidence gathering through governed investment judgement and a transaction
decision, preserving decision-time state. Cycle: Source or screen, assess, diligence, value, recommend, decide,
transact or stop, learn. It is deliberately lifecycle-level pending the Investment workshop.

## The Situation
The Invest build runs Origination to Ownership on one Situation: a single opportunity record that carries the
Company, evidence, evaluations, decisions and later ownership reporting from first sight to exit. One Company may
have several Situations over time.

## Stages
1. Origination: the opportunity is captured and the Company resolved.
2. Screening: first-pass evaluation against the Investment Opportunity Evaluation framework; candidate outcome
   progress, investigate, watch or eliminate. Screening decision by the Investment team.
3. Diligence: structured research and reconciliation (`LP-14`).
4. Valuation: valuation and return scenarios with sensitivities.
5. Recommendation: framework evaluation and recommendation pack.
6. Decision: governed human investment decision, recorded with frozen basis (`LP-10`).
7. Transaction or stop: authorised through `LP-15`; executed by people and advisers, not by AQL.
8. Ownership: periodic ownership reporting against the investment case; hold, add or exit decisions.
9. Learning: outcomes compared with the frozen case (`LP-06`).

## Decision gates and reserved decisions
Progressing or eliminating at screening, the investment decision, transaction approval, and later hold or exit
decisions are reserved human decisions. Intelligence supports them and never silently executes them.

## Reapproval
Material new evidence after an approval triggers reapproval through `LP-15` before transaction.

## Evaluation of AQL itself
`LP-05` evaluates AQL's technical and control quality on this work, never investment merit.

## Confidentiality
Situation material, especially data-room content, is confidential to the Situation's Work Context.

## Open items
Stage definitions, gate owners, required artefacts per stage, timing rules, ownership reporting measures.
