# Financial Health framework (proposed)

## Status
Workshop discovery. This framework will define how Halience evaluates the financial health and resilience of a
company. It is reusable: investment evaluation (the Financial dimension), acquisition onboarding, weekly operating
reports, operating health and portfolio review may consume it rather than duplicate financial methodology.

## What it will contain
Per the Domain Evaluation structure: dimension, description, criterion or measure, evaluation method, benchmark or
reference, threshold or rule, weight, evidence required, confidence or sufficiency rule, owner.

## Candidate areas (to confirm, not settled)
- Revenue quality and growth.
- Profitability and margins.
- Cash generation, burn and runway.
- Liquidity and working capital.
- Balance-sheet strength and leverage.
- Forecast reliability.

## To be decided
- The dimensions and metrics, with exact definitions and periods.
- Benchmarks and reference sets, by sector and stage.
- Thresholds and whether any metric is scored or only judged.
- Weights, if any.
- Evidence requirements: which sources (bank, ledger, management accounts, filed accounts) suffice for which measure.
- Confidence and sufficiency rules, including how stale or unreconciled data affects a judgement.

## Until decided
Consumers present measures with the definition used and their trend, without ratings or thresholds, and state that
the Financial Health framework is not yet defined. Missing data lowers confidence; it is never read as zero.
