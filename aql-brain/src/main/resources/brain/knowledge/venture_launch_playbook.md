# Founders' launch playbook (structure)

## Status
Proposed placeholder. This file sets out the structure the Build build uses to launch a venture milestone by
milestone. The stage content, gate criteria, thresholds and approvers are to be written by the founders. Until they
are, AQL treats every gate as lacking agreed criteria and asks the gate owner what will be judged.

## Stages and gates
Each stage lists: purpose; typical milestones; evidence the next gate needs; who approves the gate.

### Stage 0: Commission
- Purpose: decide whether to start.
- Milestones: commission brief, initial research, first-stage scope.
- Gate G0, commission: is the thesis worth testing, and is the first-stage scope and budget acceptable?

### Stage 1: Discover
- Purpose: test the riskiest assumptions with customers and the market.
- Milestones: customer conversations, problem evidence, alternatives, positioning draft, regulatory check.
- Gate G1, build decision: is there enough evidence of need to build a first product?

### Stage 2: Build
- Purpose: build the minimum product and the site.
- Milestones: product build, site build, content, analytics, operational readiness (support, payments, legal pages).
- Gate G2, launch readiness: does it work, is it tested, are claims compliant, are operations ready?

### Stage 3: Launch
- Purpose: put the venture in front of customers.
- Milestones: approved launch content published, channels live, first measures captured.
- Launch publishing goes through the supervised GTM loop (`LP-04`) with human approval.

### Stage 4: Observe and adjust
- Purpose: learn from early customers and fix what matters.
- Milestones: weekly measures, material-change review, interventions.
- Gate G3, scale, change or stop: is the evidence strong enough to scale, should the venture change course, or
  should it stop?

## Rules that apply whatever the content
- Every gate is a governed human decision with frozen basis and named approvers.
- Coding and site-build work happens in an authorised workspace within an approved scope; going live is a separate
  approved step.
- Customer-facing claims follow gtm_content_rules and the regulated perimeter.

## For the founders to decide
Stage names and order; milestones per stage; gate criteria and thresholds; approvers per gate; budget per stage;
default measures after launch; what triggers an early stop.
