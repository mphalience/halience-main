# Venture lifecycle (LP-19)

## Purpose
`LP-19` moves a venture from commissioning through delivery and launch into evidence-based scale, change or stop
decisions. Cycle: Commission, discover or build, assess, launch, observe, intervene, scale, change or stop, learn.

## The Venture object
A venture created and developed through the Halience venture studio. It may exist before any legal Company and may
later create or become associated with one. Venture and Company are distinct.

## Stages
1. Commission: a commission brief sets thesis, evidence, scope and initial milestones; the commission is a gate.
2. Discover and build: research (`LP-14`), human and agent work (`LP-09`), agent runs (`LP-17`) including coding and
   site build in an authorised workspace.
3. Assess: readiness against gate criteria.
4. Launch: a governed gate; go-to-market through the supervised GTM loop (`LP-04`).
5. Observe: monitoring (`LP-03`) and attention (`LP-08`) on post-launch evidence.
6. Intervene: changes routed as work.
7. Scale, change or stop: a governed gate.
8. Learn: outcomes through `LP-06`.

The Build build realises this milestone by milestone, using the founders' launch playbook (venture_launch_playbook)
for stage and gate structure.

## Governance
- Stage gates and scale or stop decisions remain governed human decisions, approved through `LP-15` and recorded with
  their basis through `LP-10`.
- Exact thresholds and processes are not inherited as canonical until the Venture Studio specifies them.
- Gate assessment is independent of the work being assessed.

## Chain
`CH-07`: `LP-19` composes `LP-14`, `LP-09`, `LP-15` at gates, launch, `LP-03`, `LP-08`, `LP-06`. Specialist loops return
state to `LP-19`, which owns lifecycle and stage state.

## Open items
Gate criteria, thresholds and approvers per gate; venture performance measures after launch.
