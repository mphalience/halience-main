# Strategy and planning (LP-11)

## Purpose
`LP-11` connects objectives, scenarios and resource choices to execution evidence and replanning. Cycle: Objectives,
scenarios, prioritise, plan, execute, measure, replan. It is directional until strategy and planning scope is agreed.

## Objects
- Objective: a longer-term intended outcome for a governed scope. Not work.
- Priority: what matters most now within a context. Influences sequencing and attention.
- Project, Work Block, Task: the work that executes a plan (see human_agent_work).
- Decision: material allocation and strategy choices, recorded with their basis (see decision_lineage).

## Method
1. Objectives: owner, measure, horizon.
2. Scenarios: a small number of distinct futures or options, each with explicit assumptions. Defined quantities
   (budget, capacity, forecast) are calculations; interpretation is analysis.
3. Prioritise: compare options on common dimensions, showing trade-offs and what each defers.
4. Plan: Projects and Work Blocks with owners, sequencing, checkpoints and measures.
5. Execute through `LP-09`; monitor through `LP-03` and `LP-08`.
6. Measure: compare outcome with plan; attribute variance where evidence allows.
7. Replan from current evidence without rewriting the basis of prior decisions.

## Composition
`LP-14` for research; `LP-10` for material decisions; `LP-15` for consequential allocation; `LP-06` for outcome
learning (chain `CH-09`). Loop-back to replan is deliberate, not circular.

## Human ownership
Material allocation and strategy decisions remain human-owned unless explicitly delegated. AQL presents options and
plans with their basis; it does not choose between them.
