# Investment Opportunity Evaluation framework

## Purpose
Defines how Halience evaluates whether an opportunity is attractive and suitable for investment. AQL owns the
evaluation mechanics; the Investment team defines what good means. Status: workshop discovery. Every criterion below
is a candidate for validation.

## How to read this framework
- Weights and thresholds are SME homework. None are set yet; where none are configured, evaluation is a reasoned
  judgement that says no threshold was applied.
- Not every criterion is scored. Methods include quantitative metrics, qualitative judgement, binary eligibility or
  exclusion rules, benchmark comparison, thresholds, scoring or weighted scoring.
- Missing evidence lowers confidence; it does not silently count against the opportunity.

## Dimensions and criteria
Each criterion: evaluation method / evidence required / confidence or sufficiency rule.

### Investment Fit: alignment with thesis, mandate, criteria and exclusions
- Thesis / mandate fit: qualitative judgement plus eligibility and exclusion rules / thesis, mandate, company profile,
  opportunity context / evidence sufficient to determine fit and identify material uncertainty.
- Portfolio fit / interaction: qualitative judgement / current portfolio, planned investments, strategic priorities /
  relevant portfolio context available. Covers complementarity, overlap, conflicts and synergies.
- Investment structure fit: eligibility and exclusion rules plus qualitative judgement / proposed size, stage,
  ownership, transaction structure / core proposed terms or assumptions available.

### Company: quality and attractiveness of the company itself
- Business model and proposition: qualitative judgement / business model, proposition, customer and commercial
  evidence / sufficient to understand how the company creates and captures value.
- Traction and competitive position: metric review, qualitative judgement, benchmark comparison / traction, customer,
  product and competitor evidence / sufficient to distinguish demonstrated traction from unsupported claims.
- Leadership, ownership and governance: qualitative judgement / leadership, ownership, governance, key-person
  evidence / material leadership and ownership gaps identified.

### Market: attractiveness of the market and category
- Market opportunity: quantitative measures plus benchmark comparison / market size, growth, category evidence /
  material assumptions, source quality and recency visible.
- Market dynamics and structural trends: qualitative judgement plus evidence synthesis / trends, demand drivers,
  external conditions / material uncertainty and conflicting evidence surfaced.
- Competitive intensity and barriers: qualitative judgement plus benchmark comparison / competitor, substitution and
  barrier-to-entry evidence / competitive landscape sufficiently evidenced.

### Financial: financial quality, health and performance (may consume financial_health_framework)
- Growth and revenue quality: quantitative measures, trend and benchmark comparison / historical and current revenue,
  growth, concentration, recurring or repeat revenue / evidence sufficient for the metric and period judged.
- Profitability, cash generation and resilience: quantitative measures, scenario and benchmark comparison / margins,
  profitability, cash flow, burn, runway, balance sheet / key gaps and accounting or forecast assumptions visible.
- Capital efficiency: quantitative measures plus benchmark comparison / capital raised and deployed, growth or output
  evidence / comparable measures and assumptions available.

### Valuation: attractiveness relative to value and proposed price or terms
- Proposed valuation / price: valuation analysis plus benchmark comparison / proposed price or terms and Company
  Valuation outputs / valuation basis and material assumptions available.
- Valuation attractiveness: comparative judgement plus sensitivity analysis / Company Valuation, comparables,
  performance, proposed terms / material valuation uncertainty visible.

### Investment Returns: expected return profile
- Return scenarios: scenario analysis / Investment Modelling outputs and scenario assumptions / base, downside and
  upside assumptions explicit.
- Sensitivity and downside: sensitivity and downside analysis / modelling sensitivities and material assumptions /
  material drivers and downside exposure understood.
- Exit / realisation assumptions: scenario analysis plus qualitative judgement / exit assumptions, holding period,
  market evidence / key dependencies and uncertainty explicit.

### Value Creation: potential for Halience to improve value and influence the outcome (candidate new dimension)
- Value-creation levers: qualitative judgement plus quantified opportunity where supportable / company evidence,
  strategic analysis, identified opportunities / material levers evidenced rather than assumed.
- Magnitude and time to impact: scenario and impact assessment / estimated impact, timing, dependencies, assumptions /
  material assumptions and uncertainty explicit.
- Halience ability to influence: qualitative judgement / ownership and rights, capabilities, resources, execution
  context / degree of influence and dependencies understood.

### Risk: material risks, likelihood, impact and implications
- Material risk profile: risk assessment by configured risk categories (commercial, financial, market, operational,
  technical and product, legal and regulatory, people, transaction) / evidence in each category / material known
  risks and evidence gaps identified. Categories are configured, not mandatory scoring dimensions.
- Risk severity and implications: likelihood and impact plus qualitative judgement / evidence for each material risk /
  severity and uncertainty distinguishable.
- Red flags / override conditions: binary or conditional rules where appropriate / evidence for any configured
  red-flag condition / no automatic override unless explicitly configured.

### Evidence / Confidence: evaluation-wide, not an attractiveness score
- Evidence coverage and quality: coverage and source-quality assessment / evidence across all dimensions / required
  evidence gaps visible.
- Freshness and contradictions: freshness and conflict assessment / source dates, conflicting evidence,
  reconciliation state / stale or unresolved conflicting evidence visible.
- Overall confidence / sufficiency: confidence and sufficiency judgement / dimension-level evidence and confidence /
  framework distinguishes sufficient evidence from unknown or insufficient evidence.

## Framework outcome
- Overall judgement and recommendation: holistic judgement using configured rules and domain reasoning / completed
  dimension evaluations, evidence, risks, uncertainties / must preserve rationale, confidence and material unknowns.
- Rationale, material risks and evidence gaps: explainable synthesis / supporting evidence and dimension-level
  judgements / material claims traceable to evidence.
- Next evidence or action required: gap-driven recommendation / open gaps, unresolved risks, decision state / next
  step reflects what would materially change or advance the judgement.

Candidate outcomes: progress, investigate, watch, eliminate, or equivalent configured outcomes. The outcome is a
Recommendation. The investment decision remains a governed human decision (`LP-18`).

## For SMEs to decide
Validate the dimension and criterion list; set weights, thresholds and benchmarks; choose which criteria are scored
and which are judged; define red-flag conditions and whether any override; configure risk categories; confirm the
outcome set; state which criteria apply at screening versus full diligence.
