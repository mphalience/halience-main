# Operating health and management (LP-20)

## Purpose
`LP-20` uses governed operating evidence to identify and respond to material company health, risk, capacity and
coordination issues. Cycle: Observe, assess, diagnose, respond, measure, learn. Directional until Ops discovery.

## Health areas
- Finance: cash, runway, revenue, costs, working capital (see financial_health_framework).
- Risk and compliance: open Risks and Issues, regulatory deadlines, obligations.
- Capacity and coordination: planned versus committed work, blocked dependencies, overdue commitments, at team or work
  level.
- Suppliers: dependency, contract and performance state.
- Knowledge: staleness of governed Knowledge the company relies on.

## Method (chain CH-08)
Monitoring (`LP-03`) assesses material change; attention (`LP-08`) decides who needs involvement; research (`LP-14`)
supports diagnosis; work (`LP-09`) and agent runs (`LP-17`) carry the response; consequential responses go through
`LP-15`; outcomes feed `LP-06` and the next assessment.

## Non-surveillance (GV-22)
Operational visibility supports legitimate coordination and outcomes. It must not turn private Halie context,
activity volume or behavioural telemetry into employee surveillance.
- Use work, commitment, dependency, outcome and business state.
- Do not use message counts, online time, keystrokes, private conversations or personal activity measures.
- Do not rank, score or profile individual employees.
- Capacity is a property of plans and commitments, not of watching people.

## Visibility
Role and authority scoped (`GV-18`). A health view never exposes content its viewer cannot otherwise see.

## Materiality
A raw change is not an exception until it passes configured rules (`GV-13`). Where thresholds are not agreed, show
measures and trends without a rating.

## Open items
Metrics and thresholds per area (Ops discovery), review cadence, escalation owners.
