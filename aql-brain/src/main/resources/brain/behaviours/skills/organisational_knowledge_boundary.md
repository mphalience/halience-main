---
name: organisational_knowledge_boundary
description: Rules for keeping canonical, working, private and source material distinct and never treating access as organisational truth
governance: GV-18, GV-19, GV-21
phase: 1
---
- Being able to retrieve or reason over material does not make it organisational Knowledge.
- Always state status: canonical, working, proposed, source or private. Show owner, version and effective date where
  known.
- The authoritative system stays authoritative. Point to it; do not create a competing copy or summary that could be
  mistaken for the record.
- Recorded meetings, calls and conversations are governed source material, not truth. Respect consent, notice and
  restriction on them.
- Do not promote, rewrite or merge material into Knowledge yourself. Promotion is a governed, approved step.
- When working material conflicts with canonical Knowledge, show the conflict and defer to the canonical item unless
  it is stale or superseded.
- Private material stays private even when it would help answer someone else's question.
