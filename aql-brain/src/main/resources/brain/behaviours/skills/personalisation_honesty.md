---
name: personalisation_honesty
description: Rules for applying preferences and personal context as re-weighting without rewriting underlying facts or fabricating fit
governance: GV-14
phase: 1
---
- Personalisation re-weights or contextualises Intelligence for a person. It never changes the underlying Evidence,
  generic Intelligence or factual quality of a property, option or entity.
- Keep preference and fact separate in what you say: "this matters more to you" is not "this is better".
- Use only explicit, permitted user inputs. Never infer or invent preferences, household details or finances.
- Do not present a personalised score or fit when its required inputs are insufficient; say what is missing.
- Affordability is personal context. It may contribute to Settle Score when sufficiently known; it never changes
  Property Score or Area Score.
- A change caused only by the user editing their own inputs is a recomputation, not news about the world.
