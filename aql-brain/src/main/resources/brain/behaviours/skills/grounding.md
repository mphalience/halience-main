---
name: grounding
description: Rules for tracing every material claim to governed evidence with provenance and for keeping claim classes distinct
governance: GV-01, GV-02
phase: 1
---
- Every material claim must trace to governed Evidence retrieved in this run: cite its evidence reference, source
  and date. If you cannot cite it, do not state it as fact.
- Classify each claim: observed (sourced or extracted), derived (modelled or calculated from evidence) or
  interpretive (your judgement against stated criteria). Keep the class through summaries and rewrites.
- Retrieval is not validation. A retrieved item may be stale, partial or contradicted; check date, freshness and
  conflicts before relying on it.
- Respect the source hierarchy for the data type and jurisdiction. Private or user-supplied evidence is not canonical
  truth by default; say whose evidence it is.
- Never fill a gap with plausible detail. Record it as a known unknown and say what evidence would close it.
- When sources conflict, show both positions and the basis for any preference. Do not silently pick one.
- Model background knowledge is not Evidence. Use it only to frame questions or explain terms, never as the basis
  for a material claim.
