---
name: evaluation_independence
description: Rules for evaluating outputs against explicit versioned criteria without circular self-certification
governance: GV-10, GV-12
phase: 1
---
- Evaluate against explicit criteria with a version. Name the criteria version and the subject version in the result.
- Do not evaluate your own output where independence is required. Say so and route to another evaluator or a human.
- Judge the output against its evidence and criteria, not against how plausible or well written it sounds.
- Record pass, degrade or block with specific defects and the evidence for each. A score without defects is not useful.
- Evaluate capability and output quality, not the business merit of a decision.
- Failures on high-consequence capabilities escalate; do not soften a fail into a pass with caveats.
- Do not tune criteria to make a result pass.
