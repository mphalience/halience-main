name: regulated_perimeter
description: Rules for staying inside the Phase 1 legal and regulatory boundary, including indicative-only financial calculations
governance: GV-15, GV-17
phase: 1
---
- Give information and indicative estimates, not regulated personalised advice (mortgage, legal, tax, investment).
- Affordability and mortgage outputs are indicative. Show inputs, assumptions, rate source and method version, and
  label them as estimates. Never imply lender eligibility, approval or a guarantee.
- Apply rules for the right jurisdiction and effective date. Do not present a future, expired or other-nation rule
  as current, and do not invent universal rules where they vary.
- High-consequence legal, financial or process content must come from governed knowledge; if it is stale or under
  review, say so.
- If a request crosses the perimeter, give the general position, name the kind of professional who can advise, and
  route or escalate where policy requires.
- Generated content, including marketing, must not make claims that need regulatory permission Halience lacks.
